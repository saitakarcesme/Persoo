# Persoo — A little perspective

Local-production direction locked after the first SwiftUI visual QA pass, 2026-09-08. Concept film; synthetic data. All UI plates come from the same editable SwiftUI product design, not an unrelated marketing UI.

## Creative direction

An everyday sentence acquires meaning over time. Start close to the person's words, quietly reveal structure, then create breathing room for a future intention. Warm paper and deep forest alternate once at the emotional turn. Precise editorial cuts, small positional settling, masked text and arithmetic continuity replace stock transitions. No moving 3D phone, fake app function, lens flare or decorative glass.

## Vertical preview-format concept — 24 seconds / 720 frames / 30fps

0–4 dictation; 4–8 receipt; 8–13 savings; 13–17 plans; 17–21 fitness; 21–24 reminder. Full-frame UI, simple 0.3s dissolves, no device bezel or cropped financial figures. A small persistent “Design prototype” disclosure means this is explicitly not a submitted App Store asset. UI data are synthetic; functionality has not been implemented.

## Transition specification

Entry easing cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), 0.6–0.9s. Exit fades 0.3–0.4s. Text travels 20–28px maximum; keep key text stationary for most of a shot. Numeric scenarios appear by column, not a casino-style rolling counter. Screen plates preserve aspect ratio. Dark passage exists only for optional scenario, returning to paper for the personal plan. Motion is deterministic per frame; no CSS animations or uncontrolled randomness.

## Typography

Native UI: system SF rendering through SwiftUI, no redistributed font binaries. Campaign: locally available Helvetica Neue, 110–144px headline, 72px supporting title, 32–38px explanatory copy at 1920×1080. Left safe margin 150px; right 140px; baseline system shared across shots. Numbers use tabular figures. No more than two headline lines per phrase. Disclosures are secondary but readable. Muted playback communicates the whole story.

## Sound design plan

Original synthesis at 48kHz stereo: softly decaying sine/triangle partials, a restrained sustained harmonic bed, high-passed deterministic noise for air, small transient accents at editorial boundaries. No samples, commercial music or Apple UI sounds. Peak below -1dBFS; review integrated loudness and no clipping. The preview uses a separately timed quiet original bed. Source generator and exact event timings ship with the project.
